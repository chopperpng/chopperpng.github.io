ABOUT
ME

I'm interested in understanding how games work — from the systems underneath them to the way they look, sound and feel.

I'm currently entering my second year of a BSc (Hons) Game Technology degree at Birmingham City University.

During my first year I've worked with Unity, Unreal Engine 5, C++, Blueprints, Maya and game audio. I've also explored game design, modelling, animation and rapid development through game jams.

Outside of development I'm heavily interested in games, music, cinema, art and expressionism, as well as computer hardware and understanding how the technology behind the things I use actually works.
